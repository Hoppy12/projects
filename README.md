# Projects by Thomas Hopgood
A collection of projects I've completed. Short descriptions of each are supplied below

## Perturbations of the Einstein Cylinder
This project was completed as part of the UC Summer Research program in the 2025/2026 semester. We first outline the introduction of a spinor formalism of general relativity. We then define the Newman-Penrose coefficients and explain how they are used in perturbation theory to obtain the Teukolsky equation. We then apply this method to the Einstein cylinder, a geometry not of Petrov type D.

## Conformal Gauss Gauge (CGG) in Minkowski Spacetime
The conformal Gauss gauge is a convenient way to transport quantities along conformal geodesics. In particular, the conformal factor associated with such a congruence is quadratic in parameter time and the location of the conformal boundary is known in advance. The differential equations resulting from the gauge are typically quite complicated and numerical methods are required. However, in Minkowski space, these equations can be solved analytically after a bit of effort.

## Cell Packing Geometry
This is a project completed for the 2024/2025 UC Summer Research program. It investigates what affect tension and contractility have on trends in the geometry of cell formation. I outline here how statistical analysis of simulated cell structures yields information about the parameters used.
